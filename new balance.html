Product Details

New Balance 550 is a retro basketball sneaker from 1989, reissued as a lifestyle staple for its clean, 80s court look. It has a low-top leather upper with perforated toe box, synthetic overlays, and the classic 'N' logo on the side. The chunky sole uses a rubber cupsole with pivot circles for that vintage hoops feel, plus EVA foam for lightweight cushioning. Known for crisp colorways like white/green, white/red, and white/navy, the 550 is big in streetwear for its timeless, off-court aesthetic. Made for casual wear now, not actual basketball.
