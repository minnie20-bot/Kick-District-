Product Details

Nike Gato is an indoor soccer shoe designed for futsal, built low to the ground for court control and touch. It has a soft leather or synthetic leather upper with minimal overlays, a textured toe for ball grip, and a small Swoosh on the side. The midsole is very thin with basic foam for cushioning, sitting on a gum rubber outsole with a herringbone tread pattern for grip on indoor surfaces. Sleek and minimalist, it’s known for classic colorways like white/black and black/white, and has crossed into streetwear as a casual low-profile sneaker. Made for futsal but often worn casually.
